Welcome to WatchNext

WatchNext is a platform where you can find your movies and book your cinema tickets in one place. Whether you want a funny movie or a thriller, we can help you decide what to watch next.

Our purpose

We exist to make going to the movies simple and enjoyable for everyone for everyone. Choosing a movie can be hard when there are so many options, and booking tickets can be a hassle. WatchNext solves both problems by bringing browsing, recommendations and booking together on one easy website.

Our values are convienience, variety, accessability and entertainment

Who Is It For?

WatchNext is made for everyone who loves movies.

  • Families looking for a movie everyone can enjoy
  • Couples planning a date night
  • Students who want an easy and affordable night out
  • Movie enthusiasts who always want to find somehting new

What we Offer

Here is what you can do on our website

  • Movie discovery: browse the movies that are showing
  • Personalised recommendations: tell us what you like and we suggest movies related to that
  • Easy ticket booking; choose your movie, session and tickets in a few steps
